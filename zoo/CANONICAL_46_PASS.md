## Composite verdict

**CONDITIONALLY SUPPORTED as a theorem/audit program.** The Zoo strengthens provenance, falsification, identifiability and anti-overclaim discipline. It does not convert formal consequences into empirical laws without data.

The most consequential Zoo outputs were CAT (corollary compression), BAT/RAVEN/LYNX (novelty and baseline attacks), MAGPIE/ELEPHANT/SALMON (provenance), PENGUIN (precision limits), and WEASEL/MANTIS/BISON (degeneracy and domain-boundary attacks).
