# New Derivations: T177–T181

These five results emerged from the post-156 Zoo audit. They are **DERIVED — LEAN PENDING** in this package.

## T177 — Scalar Coupling Sign Degeneracy

Given

\[
v_\phi(\alpha)=\frac{c}{\sqrt{1-\alpha^2}},
\]

the dependence is only on \(\alpha^2\). Therefore

\[
\boxed{v_\phi(-\alpha)=v_\phi(\alpha)}.
\]

**Interpretation:** phase-speed measurements constrain \(|\alpha|\), not the sign of \(\alpha\).

---

## T178 — Real-Wave Admissibility

Start from the source-free plane-wave dispersion relation

\[
(1-\alpha^2)\omega^2=c^2k^2.
\]

For a nontrivial real wave with \(c\neq0\), \(k\neq0\), and \(\omega\neq0\), both \(c^2k^2\) and \(\omega^2\) are positive. Hence

\[
1-\alpha^2>0
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{\alpha^2<1}.
\]

**Interpretation:** the \(\alpha^2<1\) phase-speed domain is forced by nontrivial real plane-wave propagation in this formalized equation.

## T179 — Scalar Coupling Inversion

If

\[
v=\frac{c}{\sqrt{1-\alpha^2}},
\]

then

\[
\frac{c^2}{v^2}=1-\alpha^2,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{\alpha^2=1-\left(\frac{c}{v}\right)^2}.
\]

Thus

\[
|\alpha|=\sqrt{1-(c/v)^2}.
\]

The sign ambiguity of T177 remains.

For small \(|\alpha|\),

\[
\frac{v-c}{c}\approx\frac{\alpha^2}{2}.
\]

## T180 — Cosmic Rotation Sign Degeneracy

For

\[
H^2_{\rm EFMW}=H^2_{\Lambda{\rm CDM}}+\frac{\Omega_U^2}{a^2},
\]

only \(\Omega_U^2\) appears. Therefore

\[
\boxed{H^2_{\rm EFMW}(\Omega_U)=H^2_{\rm EFMW}(-\Omega_U)}.
\]

**Interpretation:** expansion history alone cannot recover the orientation/sign of the proposed residual rotation from this term.

---

## T181 — Curvature–Rotation Degeneracy

The baseline curvature and proposed rotation contributions are

\[
-\frac{k}{a^2}+\frac{\Omega_U^2}{a^2}
=
-\frac{k-\Omega_U^2}{a^2}.
\]

Define

\[
k_{\rm eff}=k-\Omega_U^2.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
H^2_{\rm EFMW}(k,\Omega_U)
=
H^2_{\Lambda{\rm CDM}}(k_{\rm eff})
}.
\]

**Interpretation:** within this currently formalized Friedmann expression, constant residual rotation is exactly degenerate with a shift in the curvature parameter. Expansion-history data alone therefore cannot identify \(\Omega_U\) independently of \(k\).

This is a confounding/identifiability theorem, not empirical confirmation of rotation.
