# Candidate-Law Assessment

## 1. Scalar propagation relation — strong candidate physical relation

\[
(1-\alpha^2)\omega^2=c^2k^2,
\qquad
v_\phi=\frac{c}{\sqrt{1-\alpha^2}}.
\]

### Current strengths
- direct mathematical consequence of the formalized scalar equation;
- yields a sharp nonzero-coupling phase-speed prediction;
- finite-precision measurements can bound the coupling;
- T177–T179 sharpen identifiability and domain structure.

### Current limitations
- formal consequence of a postulated equation is not evidence that nature follows it;
- phase velocity is not automatically information/front velocity;
- sign of \(\alpha\) is not identified by phase speed;
- empirical confirmation remains outstanding.

**Status:** CANDIDATE PHYSICAL RELATION — NOT ESTABLISHED LAW.

---

## 2. Residual-rotation Friedmann relation — candidate with a major degeneracy

\[
\Delta H^2=\frac{\Omega_U^2}{a^2}.
\]

### Current strengths
- explicit, signed prediction: contribution is nonnegative;
- null measurements give a quantitative \(\Omega_U\) bound.

### Major new limitation
T181 shows exact curvature degeneracy:

\[
k_{\rm eff}=k-\Omega_U^2.
\]

Expansion history by itself therefore cannot distinguish constant residual rotation from a curvature shift.

A viable test needs an independently curvature-sensitive or rotation-sensitive observable.

**Status:** CANDIDATE PHYSICAL RELATION — IDENTIFIABILITY PROBLEM OPEN.

---

## 3. Recursive coherence/control principle — candidate systems principle

The formalization connects self-model tracking, coherence tending to one, and no-warning behavior under perfect self-modeling assumptions.

The separate affine-survivor tranche gives perturbation propagation

\[
\delta_{n+1}=(1-\alpha\beta)\delta_n.
\]

This is useful control mathematics but is close to standard contraction theory. Empirical advantage over conventional monitors must be established by matched baselines and replication.

**Status:** CANDIDATE SYSTEMS PRINCIPLE — NOT A NEW FUNDAMENTAL LAW OF PHYSICS.

---

## Novelty verdict

The post-156 audit did **not** justify promoting a fifth or sixth fundamental physical law.

Its most valuable new output is the identification of:
- sign degeneracies;
- domain/admissibility constraints;
- inverse parameter mappings;
- curvature/rotation confounding;
- explicit boundaries on what experiments can infer.
